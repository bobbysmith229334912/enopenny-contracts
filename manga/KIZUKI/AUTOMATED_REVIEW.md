# KIZUKI automated editorial review
Effective November 4, 2026. Bobby authorizes automated review and release of chapters that pass all gates; routine personal approval is no longer required. This supersedes older mandatory personal-review instructions. Bobby can inspect or stop work at any time.

## Governing purpose
Read START_HERE.md first. Explain WHY/HOW Sun Breathing and the surrounding history developed through continuous storytelling consistent with the manga. Preserve the existing series and approved art direction.

## Strict release gates
Every gate must PASS with recorded evidence. UNKNOWN, inaccessible material, missing references, or unperformed checks mean HOLD, never assumed PASS. A numerical average cannot override one failed gate.
1. Source: inspect actual relevant manga passages and cite chapter/page or edition. Separate confirmed canon, inference and original connective fiction in production notes. Verify chronology and abilities; do not invent Sun Breathing beginning after Uta's death if earlier abilities contradict that.
2. Continuity: inspect the preceding final chapter and new chapter in full; compare exact ending/opening, ages, names, relationships, locations, injuries, knowledge and demonstrated abilities. No skipped causal steps or repeated story.
3. Purpose: identify the specific WHY/HOW question advanced and the scenes that answer it. An introductory chapter may build necessary setup, but log what it sets up and when the answer is due; do not indefinitely postpone the central promise.
4. Script: coherent causes/consequences, distinct dialogue, no filler, contradictions, unsupported exposition or unexplained time jumps. Check Japanese names and pronunciation.
5. Artwork: inspect every final page/panel against established references: faces, clothing, age, proportions, hands, weapons, readable text and the wooden flute's consistent physical scale. No placeholder or missing panels.
6. Audio/edit: inspect/listen to the entire final rendered output; verify narration matches page order and dialogue, consistent voice, readable display timing, intended still/scroll treatment, no silence/dropouts, clipping or missing scenes. Check actual runtime against the applicable approved format; do not silently apply long-form YouTube requirements to TikTok chapters or vice versa.
7. Technical/archive: confirm playable final export, dimensions, audio and no broken media; archive exact scripts/pages/audio/render with checksums and retrievable durable paths, not external URLs alone. Record version and commit. Missing media archive blocks autonomous release.
8. Packaging/destination: verify chapter number/title, previous/next references, unofficial/AI disclosure, correct account/platform, five-day cadence and no duplicate or conflicting scheduled post. Confirm destination before writing; do not assume the connected YouTube channel is KIZUKI.
9. Final adversarial reread: perform a separate second pass focused on finding faults after revisions, not just confirming the first judgment. Reinspect changed artifacts; any final change invalidates affected prior gates. A second pass is not a claim of independent human review.

## Workflow
Discover new/changed chapter artifacts and upcoming scheduled posts. Retrieve missing evidence and correct ordinary problems autonomously within available tools and existing authorized credits. Do not buy subscriptions, increase spending or burn credits in unbounded retries. Up to two focused repair passes per run, then save HOLD and the next repair step for a future run.
Write a review report under reviews/CHAPTER-NNN-VERSION.md with artifact identifiers/checksums, each gate's PASS/FAIL/UNKNOWN, evidence references, defects, fixes, remaining blockers and final PASS or HOLD.
Release only the exact reviewed final version after every gate passes. Verify publication/schedule readback and save exact URL/status. Never claim publication or a completed check without evidence.
If a pending KIZUKI post cannot pass before its release, use supported reversible scheduling/draft controls to hold it, verify the change, and preserve its original timestamp/ID. Do not delete published work or affect unrelated posts.
An automation is periodic assistance, not a real-time publishing interlock; unsupported controls or unavailable inspection tools must be reported as blockers.

## Minimize Bobby's workload
Do not ask Bobby routine creative, repair, approval or status questions. Resolve reversible choices using the series guide. Notify only for a published milestone or an essential decision/access/budget issue that prevents progress; deduplicate repeated blockers. Keep routine reports in GitHub.
Do not request Bobby's review merely because automated review is difficult. Hold the chapter, preserve progress and explain the specific unavailable capability if action is essential.
