# KIZUKI
## Chapter 3 - The Night the Sun Went Dark

Unofficial fan-created story. Review copy - not published.

### Page 1: Previously

Kizuki. Chapter Three. The Night the Sun Went Dark. Previously: Yoriichi found a home with Uta. They were expecting a child. He left to seek a midwife, but stopped to help a grieving stranger. When he returned, no one answered.

### Page 2: The Door Opens

His hand rests against the door. Uta? The room beyond is still. Yoriichi steps inside. A moment ago, he was a husband coming home. Now, he cannot make himself take another step.

### Page 3: The Cold Hearth

Uta and their unborn child are gone. A demon has taken the life they were building together. The hearth has grown cold. Yoriichi kneels beside her. There is no enemy left for him to face. Only the silence.

### Page 4: A Promise Broken

I said I would come back. The words leave him softly. He remembers her in the doorway, waiting for his return. His hands can no longer change what happened in this room.

### Page 5: The Days Without Her

Days pass. Light crosses the floor and disappears. Yoriichi remains beside Uta, unable to say farewell. Outside, the world continues. Inside, he cannot imagine a morning that belongs to him.

### Page 6: The Visitor

At last, a traveling demon slayer finds the house. He sees the grieving man inside and kneels a short distance away. After a long silence, he speaks gently. We should lay them to rest.

### Page 7: Earth and Silence

Together, they prepare a grave. Earth falls beneath Yoriichi's hands. He has carried water to this house, gathered wood for its hearth, and imagined his child growing here. Now, he must leave them beneath the earth.

### Page 8: What He Carries

At the grave, he takes out the small wooden flute his brother made. It rests within his palm, worn by years of keeping. He closes his fingers around it. The things he loves have never needed to be grand.

### Page 9: Those Who Stand Watch

The demon slayer tells him of others who stand between demons and the sleeping world. Yoriichi listens. Until now, his extraordinary strength had seemed unnecessary to the life he wanted. He asks where he should go.

### Page 10: The Last Look Back

He gathers his few belongings. The little flute goes safely inside his clothing. At the doorway, he looks back once. He cannot promise to return to the life he knew. He can still choose what he does next.

### Page 11: A Different Purpose

Among the demon slayers, Yoriichi watches the movement of a sword. His eyes follow the breathing, the shift of weight, the instant before a strike. The gift he once feared using has found a terrible purpose.

### Page 12: Beyond This House

He could not save the home he loved. Beyond these mountains, other doors are waiting for someone to return. Yoriichi lifts the practice sword. To be continued. Chapter Four: The First Breath of the Sun.
