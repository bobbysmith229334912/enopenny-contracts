# KIZUKI — Chapter 4: The First Breath of the Sun

Unofficial fan-created connecting scenes; not a claim that all dialogue or training sequences appear in manga canon.

## Page 01 — PREVIOUSLY • THE NIGHT THE SUN WENT DARK

Yoriichi's home has fallen silent. He has buried Uta and their unborn child. A demon slayer has guided him toward others who fight the darkness. Now Yoriichi stands among them, holding a practice sword. His grief remains, but so does his wish that another family might be spared.

## Page 02 — THE MEN WHO STAND WATCH

In the training yard, the demon slayers repeat their forms. They strike until their arms shake and their breathing breaks. Yoriichi watches quietly. Each warrior is brave. Each moves differently. And each is fighting with a body that tires.

## Page 03 — WHAT THE EYE CAN SEE

Yoriichi notices the things others cannot: the rise of the ribs, the shift of a hip, the tension before a strike. He has perceived such details since childhood. Loss has not given him this sight. It has given him a reason to share what he knows.

## Page 04 — A BREATH WITHOUT A NAME

He draws a slow breath. His shoulders remain loose. His feet settle against the earth. To Yoriichi, these movements have always felt natural. For the men watching him, they seem impossible to follow. No one has yet found the words for what he does.

## Page 05 — THE DEMONSTRATION

A practice sword moves through the air. Yoriichi steps, turns, and cuts without wasting motion. His breathing and swordsmanship form one continuous rhythm. The yard goes silent. What the others have seen cannot be learned by copying the shape of a single strike.

## Page 06 — THE LIMIT OF IMITATION

One swordsman tries to repeat the motion. His breath catches. Another loses his balance. Yoriichi understands the problem. His own body is not their body. Teaching them to become him would only leave them exhausted. He must find a different way.

## Page 07 — ONE STUDENT AT A TIME

Yoriichi kneels beside a struggling student. Instead of correcting the sword first, he studies where the man's strength begins and where it fails. A shorter step. A steadier breath. A turn fitted to his frame. At last, the student completes a clean strike.

## Page 08 — THE FIRST ADAPTATION

The student's blade flows instead of forcing its way forward. He can repeat the movement without collapsing. Another warrior favors a firm stance and a powerful blow. Yoriichi begins to change his instruction to fit the person standing before him.

## Page 09 — DIFFERENT PATHS FROM ONE LIGHT

As training continues, their strengths take distinct shapes. Some move with the continuity of water. Others favor a sudden flame-like strike, the speed of thunder, the turning force of wind, or the steadiness of stone. These are not separate miracles. They are human answers to one difficult art.

## Page 10 — WHAT THE SUN MEANS

The swordsmen need a name for the impossible source they have witnessed. Sun Breathing. The name is not the birth of Yoriichi's power. It is a way to speak of an art that was already within him, and of the techniques others are beginning to carry forward.

## Page 11 — A POWER MEANT TO BE SHARED

Yoriichi watches the students practice without him. For the first time, their movements no longer depend on copying every gesture of his own. A breath can be taught. A method can survive its teacher. His hand closes briefly around the small wooden flute hidden in his clothing.

## Page 12 — THE ROAD BEYOND THE YARD

Beyond the mountain training ground, demons still roam. The swordsmen prepare to leave together, each carrying a different way to fight. Yoriichi looks toward the distant road. He has begun to pass on the light. But one man from his past will soon step back into its shadow. To be continued.
