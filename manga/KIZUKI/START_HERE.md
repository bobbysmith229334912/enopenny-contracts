## Research questions driving the story
These are research questions, not established answers:
1. What does the manga explicitly say about Yoriichi's innate breathing, perception and swordsmanship, and what does it leave unstated about how they became a system?
2. How does Sun Breathing relate to pre-existing swordsmanship and the teaching of other swordsmen?
3. Why and how do the other breathing styles develop? What was adapted, by whom, and for what limitations?
4. How does Michikatsu's training and pursuit of his brother connect to Moon Breathing and his later choices?
5. How does Yoriichi's encounter with Muzan shape the technique's use, forms and later significance?
6. How do Sumiyoshi and his descendants preserve the knowledge, and how does it reach Tanjiro?
7. What chronology, ages, relationships, locations and demonstrated abilities constrain each proposed missing scene?

Do not invent a sudden training breakthrough after Uta's death merely because Chapter 4 has "First Breath" in its title. First verify what abilities the manga establishes Yoriichi possessed earlier. Emotional motivation, a technique's origin, its naming, its teaching and its later preservation are different questions.
